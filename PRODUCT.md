# Isola
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the user: Vite and Three.js, with plain JavaScript and locally bundled assets.

## Product Purpose
A playable 3D island sandbox with free building and roaming animals. The user confirmed this browser-based experience.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Create a living island by adding animals, nature, and buildings. Explore with orbit, pan, and zoom controls. Persist the world locally. The supplied reference video exceeds the upload transfer limit, so its content could not be inspected; no fidelity comparison is claimed.

## Product Principles
The world is visible immediately. Creation takes few actions. Wildlife brings the island to life. Actions give visible feedback and can be undone.
